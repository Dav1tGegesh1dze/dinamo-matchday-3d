Desktop builds of the Dinamo Tbilisi stand game in 3D. No developer tools and no internet needed:
download, open, play. Results are stored on the computer itself.

## Download

| Computer | File | How to run |
|----------|------|------------|
| Mac (any, Apple chip or Intel) | `Dinamo Road to Goal 3D-<version>-universal.dmg` | Open it, drag the app into **Applications**, open it from there. The first time, see below. |
| Windows 10/11 | `Dinamo Road to Goal 3D-<version>-setup.exe` | Run the installer. If SmartScreen appears: **More info → Run anyway**. |
| Windows (no install) | `Dinamo Road to Goal 3D-<version>-win.zip` | Unzip anywhere, double-click `Dinamo Road to Goal 3D.exe`. |

**Opening it the first time on a Mac.** The app is not notarised by Apple, so macOS asks once:
- macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer: double-click the app, click **Done** on the warning, then open
  **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the message about "Dinamo Road to Goal
  3D", click **Open Anyway** and confirm with the Mac's password.
- macOS 14 or older: **right-click** the app, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.

After that it opens with a normal double-click.

## Playing

The game opens fullscreen. Register with a name, mobile number and e-mail and press Start, then
click once so the mouse controls the camera. The mouse turns the camera, **WASD** moves, **Shift**
sprints, **E** picks things up and talks. Find the shirt and boots in the dressing room and the tape
in the physio room, then talk to the coach at the tunnel mouth and answer. Run out to the centre
circle following the yellow marker, then answer three more questions to beat two defenders and the
keeper. **Restart** on the result screen goes back to registration for the next player.

**Esc** frees the mouse and pauses; click to carry on. Quit with **Cmd+Q** (Mac) or **Alt+F4**
(Windows).

## For the club

On the result screen: `Ctrl+Shift+E` downloads every attempt as JSON, including the players' phone
numbers and e-mail addresses, so winners can be contacted. `Ctrl+Shift+X` clears the leaderboard.
Please reset the board after the event, since the contact details live only on that computer.

Play one run before the event: in its first run the game picks the best quality that computer can
keep smooth.

## Known limitations

Questions are placeholders until the club supplies the real ones. The ball and the goals are simple
stand-ins until real models are found.
